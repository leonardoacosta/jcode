- [ ] 3.2 Enforce configured per-schedule and global active-run limits in the existing runner. A full limit must leave due items durably pending without losing identity; releasing capacity must wake the runner. Add tests for each limit, concurrent schedule mutations, fairness among due schedules, and no duplicate starts. Pausing blocks new admission but does not claim active tasks are stopped.
  Evidence (PARTIAL, left unchecked after review): `cron_concurrency_limits_leave_occurrences_pending` proves per-schedule and global limits over DELIVERY-IN-FLIGHT work (queue Pending or Dispatched), oldest-first fairness, no duplicate starts, identity kept while waiting, release then admit (runner nudges itself after a cron delivery). ## Known limits left by review (minor)

- A recurring item's `origin_generation` is stamped at admission, so the creator-generation revocation check does not apply to it.
- `schedule list` does not flag a persisted definition whose zone or cron no longer parses; the tick skips it silently.
- A capped schedule is inert until a real budget source exists, and each occurrence is recorded `budget_unavailable`.
- Ambient-target recurring items are queued but the ambient agent, not the delivery path, consumes them.
