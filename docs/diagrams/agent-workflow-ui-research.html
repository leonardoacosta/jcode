01 / core shift
“A scheduled item should not disappear into a timer. It should become a visible, inspectable piece of work.”

jcode already has the execution substrate: a JSON-backed queue, three delivery targets, persisted context, ambient directives, permission gates, and tested runner behavior.

The opportunity is a product layer around that substrate: status, ownership, approvals, evidence, and next actions.

01 / signal

Time, event, email reply, or failed run creates a task.

02 / inbox

One queue for due work, approvals, blocks, and escalations.

03 / policy

Risk, tools, turn limits, and approval rules are visible.

04 / run

Agent, session, child, or swarm executes with evidence.

05 / outcome

Complete, retry, handoff, ask, or schedule the next action.

02 / comparative research
Signature pattern / inbox

The work queue, not the chat list

Agent inbox research consistently moves the primary unit from conversation to actionable work. The row answers: what needs attention, why now, who owns it, and what happens next.

  • Priority is based on risk and blocked progress, not recency alone.
  • Every item exposes an explicit next action.
  • Small evidence previews keep the queue scannable.
