# Governance and risk

> Status: proposed policy model

Autonomy should follow reversibility. Read-only discovery and isolated test generation can be highly autonomous. Production changes, credentials, destructive actions, migrations, external communication, and security-sensitive work require stronger gates and often human approval.

Governance should define least-privilege tools, explicit side-effect classes, approval boundaries, privacy/redaction rules, escalation routes, audit retention, and rollback. Never treat model confidence as authorization.
